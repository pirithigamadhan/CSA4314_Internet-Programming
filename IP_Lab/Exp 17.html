How the real platform protects your vote

  • Two-step login: voter ID plus a one-time code
  • A fresh code just before the ballot opens
  • Encrypted storage of voter details and ballots
  • Receipts that prove you voted without showing who you picked

This page is a design demo. It does not encrypt anything or connect to a real voter roll.
